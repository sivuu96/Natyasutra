This Kathak composition by Guru Pali Chandra draws from both Persian and Indian influences. The Payalia Tarana is an amalgamation of cultures that showcases the nuances of ancient storytelling. Our heroine describes the adorning of her Payals or anklets, as she sets the stage for meeting her beloved. This Kathak rendition encompasses yearning, overcoming obstacles and the evergreen ferocity that has driven lovers for centuries.

Raag : Pooriya Dhanashree, Taal : Teentaal The entire program is subdivided into seven sections. Each facet is individually introduced, beginning with detailed insight into the historical relevance of the composition. These are followed by demonstrations from the Guru, culminating finally into a solo and group performance by the students, respectively.
