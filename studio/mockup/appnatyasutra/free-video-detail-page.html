Bharatanatyam Free Video

Bharatanatyam is one of the oldest classical dance traditions of India, celebrated for its sculptural geometry, expressive abhinaya, and rhythmic precision. This archive recording captures repertoire and performance elements for learners and rasikas alike.

Anita Ratnam, Founder of Arangham, is a celebrated Bharatanatyam dancer and choreographer. Her work bridges traditional repertoire with contemporary stagecraft, making classical dance accessible to global audiences.
