Natyasutra Guru Pranam 2026

Natyasutra Honours Bragha G. Bessell with Guru Pranam 2026 on Guru Purnima

In a heartfelt celebration of Guru Purnima 2026, Natyasutra Online honoured Bharatanatyam dancer, teacher, and abhinaya specialist Bragha G. Bessell through its annual Guru Pranam ceremony. The felicitation was presented by Dr. Rajashree Warrier, one of India's foremost Bharatanatyam maestros, alongside Anita Jayakumar, CEO of Natyasutra, in recognition of Bessell's extraordinary contribution to Indian classical dance over more than five decades..

Bragha Bessell is widely regarded as one of the finest exponents of Bharatanatyam of her generation, celebrated equally for her command of nritta and her rare depth in abhinaya. Trained under an illustrious lineage of gurus including Sri Mangudi Durairaj Iyer, Sri Chidambaram P.S. Kunjithapadam Pillai, Padma Shri Adyar K. Lakshman, and Padma Bhushan Smt. Kalanidhi Narayanan, she has carried forward a tradition of rigorous learning and artistic integrity across decades of performance, teaching, and choreography. Her honours include the Nritya Kalanidhi from the Music Academy, Chennai (2022), the Lifetime Achievement Award from the Trinity Arts Festival of India (2025), and the Nritya Perunjyoti from ABHAI (2026), among many others.

The Guru Pranam ceremony reflects Natyasutra's ongoing commitment to honouring the guru-shishya parampara that sustains Indian classical arts. By celebrating teachers who have given their lives to the preservation and propagation of these traditions, Natyasutra affirms that the relationship between guru and shishya remains the living core of classical dance, as vital today as it has ever been.
